# Chukwuma Nwaugha

> **Note:** This is a Wikipedia-style draft article compiled from publicly available sources. It is not published on Wikipedia. Under Wikipedia's policies, articles must meet [notability guidelines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability_(people)) and should not be [autobiographical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Autobiography); this draft is intended as a starting point and personal reference.

**Chukwuma Nwaugha** (born May 1988) is a Nigerian software engineer and technology entrepreneur. He is the founder and chief executive of **VeedoAI**, a startup building multimodal AI systems for video understanding, and the creator of several AI-powered consumer products including Audiora, Bible Links, Guekn, and WikiCube.[^1][^2][^3]

## Education

Nwaugha attended Obafemi Awolowo University in Ile-Ife, Nigeria.[^5]

## Career

Nwaugha has worked as a product and full-stack engineer, describing his focus as "building delightful AI-powered apps for the next billion users".[^2] His technical background spans JavaScript/TypeScript, Node.js, Python, React Native, Java, Swift, and Objective-C.[^5]

### VeedoAI

Nwaugha founded VeedoAI, a video-intelligence company whose stated goal is to make video "truly searchable, actionable, and interactive". The company's Atlas Engine combines multimodal AI to understand video content, comprehend context, identify relationships, and extract insights from both visual and audio elements.[^2][^6] VeedoAI Ltd was registered in the United Kingdom (company no. 16236281), with Nwaugha appointed as director on 7 February 2025.[^4][^7] Ketan Desai serves as co-founder and Chief Business Officer.[^2]

### Other products

Alongside VeedoAI, Nwaugha has built a number of independent products:[^1][^3]

- **Audiora** — an AI audio product described as letting users "listen to anything, anytime"
- **Bible Links** (bible-links.com) — an AI-powered guide to the scriptures
- **Guekn** (guekn.com) — an AI image-creation tool
- **Repurp** — a tool for repurposing video for Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, and broadcast
- **WikiCube** — an AI wiki generator that produces a citation-linked wiki for any GitHub repository

## Open source

Nwaugha is an active open-source developer under the GitHub handle **nwaughachukwuma**, with around 50 public repositories, and lists himself as a contributor to the vLLM inference engine.[^1] His published libraries and tools include `async-web-search` (an asynchronous search library covering Google, Wikipedia, and arXiv APIs), `keyv-cache` (a zero-dependency key/value wrapper over the browser Cache API), `url-exists-nodejs`, `react-native-starter`, and `svelte-element-portal`.[^1][^8] His GitHub contributions were included in the GitHub Arctic Code Vault.[^1]

## Writing and podcast

Nwaugha writes on Medium (technical articles such as "Boost Web Performance with Keyv-Cache" and essays on faith) and publishes a Substack newsletter and weekly podcast covering technology, AI, and what he calls the "agent economy", with episodes including "Why Most Founders Build Products, Not Businesses" and "The Agent Economy".[^3][^9][^10] The podcast is also distributed on Apple Podcasts.[^10]

## Personal life

Nwaugha has lived in England and lists San Francisco as his base on his public profiles.[^1][^4] He publicly identifies as a Christian and writes about faith alongside technology.[^3]
